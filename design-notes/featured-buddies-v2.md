# Featured buddy pairs and sleeve membership emblem

Requested September 12, 2026. Working templates use one membership lacrosse-head QR graphic on the right sleeve; the opposite sleeve retains its bands with no badge. The new graphic adds “Proud member of the MLBL” in chrome script. It is editable, replaceable and removable through the existing panel editor.

`membership-head-source-v1.png` is the imagegen redesign of the original `qr.png`. The generated white square is intentionally blank. `scripts/build-membership-artwork.cjs` removes only the outer white background, reads the original QR's exact 25×25 modules, and places them with a four-module quiet zone. Independently decoded both the original and final emblem and the full-size rendered right sleeve with ZXing: https://mlbl.org. No QR modules were generated by the image model. Digital verification does not represent a physical printed sample.

Working template configurations are upgraded by `upgradeSleeveArtwork` when loaded, saved or prepared as a new Printify draft. Existing front/back uploads, colors and placements remain untouched. The new sleeve style marker prevents this upgrade from undoing subsequent user edits, deletion or replacement. Old Printify transfer records and uploaded products are not changed.

Back artwork defaults behind the opaque flank stripes. The panel editor's “Place artwork behind stripes” checkbox saves per team. The same renderer serves the editor, garment preview, export and new Printify panels. Legacy transfer snapshots without this property retain their prior layer order.

Eleven fixed featured-pair sources live in `public/assets/pairs/*-v2.png`. Each imagegen edit references the original Chandler plus the corresponding team's approved character: smiling, inside arms around one another's shoulders, both full chest logos visible. Only Bombers, Bulldawgs and Queen City have full sleeves and hockey helmets; Chandler and all other teams have bare upper arms and field helmets. These are new pose illustrations based on the original references, not a promise of pixel-identical faces or crests.

`lib/buddy-roster-layout.ts` and `scripts/build-buddy-rosters.cjs` compose each back from its featured pair and the other ten original player cutouts. Middle wings are wider than top/bottom rows. Featured players are taller than every supporting player. Supporting players are normalized to a consistent cropped character height, with staggered positions. The league badge begins below the lower row's chests and overlaps only the lower part of the composition. PNGs have transparent outer backgrounds.

The earlier single-player originals, cutouts, layout source and source generation notes remain available. New pair assets are fixed per team; changing layout does not regenerate their identities.
